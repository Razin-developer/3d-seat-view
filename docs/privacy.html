Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

3D Seat View is a browser extension that shows a 3D preview of a cinema hall for a movie seat-selection page.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. The extension has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What the extension reads

When you open a seat-selection page on a supported booking site, the extension reads the seat map shown on that page: seat rows and numbers, prices, which seats are available or taken, and the screen position. This is used only inside your browser to draw the 3D hall and calculate distances and viewing angles. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What is stored

Your hall adjustment settings (seat width, row spacing, step height, screen distance, screen shape) are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. You can clear them by removing the extension or clearing its data.

Network requests

The extension makes only these requests, all to the booking sites themselves:

  • the artwork image of the movie you are booking, from the booking site's image server, to show it on the 3D screen;
  • on District, the public page of that movie, to find its poster image address.

These are ordinary requests like the ones your browser already makes when you use those sites. We operate no server and receive nothing.

Actions on the page

When you press "Find the best seat", the extension selects seats on the booking page for you. It never proceeds to payment, never books, and never enters any information.

This website

The sections above describe the extension. This website (seats.movie, also reachable at seats.radr.in) is separate: it uses Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the site, such as pages viewed, clicks and scrolling. Clarity may set cookies and record anonymised interaction data, handled under Microsoft's privacy statement. Clarity is not part of the extension and receives nothing from it. The site also loads its font from Google Fonts. The explainer video is hosted on YouTube and loads only when you press play; YouTube then handles that playback under Google's privacy policy.

Mobile waitlist

If you join the waitlist for the mobile version on this website, we store the email address you enter, the phone type you pick (Android or iPhone, optional) and the time you signed up. It is kept in a Cloudflare database and used only to tell you once when the mobile version is ready. It is not shared, sold or used for other email. To be removed, ask through sunithvs.com/contact. The extension never asks for an email address.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties. Your use of the booking sites is covered by their own privacy policies.

Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

3D Seat View is built by Sunith VS. Questions about this policy can be sent through sunithvs.com/contact.
